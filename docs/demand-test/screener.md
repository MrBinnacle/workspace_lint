Purpose: confirm the person meets the gate-1 screen before you spend their fifteen minutes and your one ask. Four questions, about sixty seconds.

A NO on question 1 or question 2 ends it. Thank them and stop. Do not send the questionnaire to a person who fails the screen — a full set of answers from the wrong population is worse than no answers, because it looks like data.

## The four questions

Copy the block below into the reply.

---

Four quick ones, about a minute:

1. Does your team keep data in Notion that somebody outside the team reads and depends on being complete? Yes or no.

2. If that data turned out to be incomplete, are you the person who answers for it? Yes or no.

3. Has an auditor, a client, or a regulator ever asked you to show that data or prove it was complete? Yes, no, or not yet.

4. Is that data still in Notion today? Or did some of it move somewhere else because Notion was not trusted for it?

---

## How to read the answers

| Pattern | Reading | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 YES, 2 YES, 3 YES | Full screen. This is the primary user in `PRODUCT.md`. | Send the questionnaire. |
| 1 YES, 2 YES, 3 "not yet" | Partial. Holds the data and the accountability, no external demand yet. | Send the questionnaire. Mark the response as partial-screen. |
| 1 YES, 2 NO | Wrong person, right organisation. | Ask who does answer for it. Do not send the questionnaire to them. |
| 1 NO | Out of population. | Thank them and stop. |
| 4 names a completed move out of Notion | This is the stop signal in gate 1. | Send the questionnaire anyway. Part 2 asks what the move cost and why. That answer is the most valuable one available. |

The last row is deliberate. A team that already left Notion is not a customer, and their reason is the strongest evidence the demand test can return. Record it.

## Record each screen

Keep one line per contact in `docs/demand-test/responses/`, whether they screened in or out. Screen-outs are the denominator. Five YES answers out of six contacts and five YES answers out of sixty contacts are different findings, and only the log distinguishes them.
