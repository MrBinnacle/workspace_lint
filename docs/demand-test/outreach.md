# Outreach — message specifications

This file specifies what each first-contact message must contain. It does not supply the words. The words are Matthew's, written in his own voice at send time.

Each spec states the purpose, the required content, the prohibited content, the length limit, and the failure mode the spec exists to prevent.

## Evidence this is built on

`docs/inputs/recruiting-sweep-2026-08-16.md` records two facts that set the strategy.

1. The strict-screen population does not post publicly about accountability for Notion-held data. Cold search does not find them.
2. Matthew has already converted this population once. He answered a Notion structural problem on Reddit, publicly, free, in one response. That response produced a scoped call with a Director of Engineering. The engagement did not close. The acquisition motion worked.

The converting artifact was a free diagnosis. It was not a question and it was not a pitch.

## The rule that governs every message

Return something before asking for anything.

A message that opens with a request spends the reader's attention before it supplies any. A message that opens with a usable answer has already paid for the attention it then asks for. The second form has a working precedent in this project's own history. The first does not.

Second rule: state that no product exists. It is true. No source code has been written. It is also the one sentence that separates this from the vendor mail these recipients delete daily.

## Message 1 — Reddit diagnosis post

**Rank: first.** This is the only channel with a working precedent in this project.

**Purpose.** Reproduce the motion that already worked. Supply a real diagnosis in public. Let the people who recognise their own workspace in it come to you.

**Must contain:**

- A concrete structural failure, named and explained. Not a category. Not "workspaces get messy."
- The mechanism that produces it, in enough detail that a reader can check it against their own workspace.
- What a reader can do about it today, without any tool.
- One line stating that a read-only CLI is under consideration and that nothing is built.

**Must not contain:**

- A request. No survey link, no DM ask, no "would anyone be interested in."
- The questionnaire, in any form.
- A product name. None has been chosen — see the name constraint in `PRODUCT.md`.

**Length.** Whatever the diagnosis needs. This is the one message where length is not the constraint; usefulness is.

**Failure mode this prevents.** A recruiting post that asks r/Notion for their time returns nothing, because the reader has no reason to spend it. The precedent case gave the answer away first.

**Note on population.** `docs/inputs/recruiting-sweep-2026-08-16.md` establishes that r/Notion is not where the strict-screen population posts. It is where at least one of them read. Replies here are evidence about framing 2, the zero-config decay report. Log them separately. A strong response here is not a YES on the coverage proof.

## Message 2 — Warm contacts

**Purpose.** Reach the screen population through people who already answer Matthew's messages.

**Must contain:**

- The single question from `PRODUCT.md` gate 1: what would you actually do if someone asked you to prove a set of Notion pages was complete and current.
- Explicit permission to answer "I would say it is fine and they would take my word for it." That answer is data. Without the permission, nobody gives it.
- A statement that no product exists and this is the gate before code is written.
- An explicit request for a referral if the recipient is out of population.

**Must not contain:**

- A description of the tool. Describing it teaches the recipient the answer you want.
- An attachment.

**Length.** Short enough to read on a phone without scrolling.

**Failure mode this prevents.** Two failures. Describing the product contaminates the answer. Omitting the referral ask wastes the contact — in a population with no public channel, a referral is worth more than an opinion.

## Message 3 — ICT Institute

The one named, reachable, closest-fit candidate the recruiting sweep produced.

**Verify before sending.** The sweep supplied no URL and no quoted passage. Confirm the site, the posts, and the author attribution first. See the provenance warning at the top of `docs/inputs/recruiting-sweep-2026-08-16.md`.

**Must contain:**

- Evidence the specific posts were read. Name the risk register and the supplier register.
- The question narrowed to their practice: when an auditor works from a register held in Notion, does anyone establish that the register shown is the whole register.
- A statement that no product exists and that a template is not being sought. They sell templates. Without this line the message reads as a customer enquiry.

**Must not contain:**

- Any suggestion that their published approach is deficient. They are a prospective source, not a prospect.

**Length.** Under 200 words.

## Order of work

1. Reddit diagnosis. The only proven channel.
2. Warm contacts. Highest reply rate, and the only cheap route to referrals.
3. ICT Institute.

Send in that order, not at once. The first replies show which wording works, and wording is cheaper to correct early.

## Excluded channels

**LinkedIn is out.** Matthew ruled it out on 2026-08-16. Do not propose it again, in any form.

**Consequence, stated so it is not discovered at contact three.** Cold outreach is now closed. Three channels remain: one public post, one warm network, one named organisation. Gate 1 in `PRODUCT.md` requires five teams. That target now depends almost entirely on the Reddit post generating inbound replies, the way the precedent case did.

Two things follow.

1. The Reddit diagnosis has to be genuinely good, and one post may not produce five screen-ins. Plan for a series over weeks, each diagnosing a different concrete failure, rather than one post and a wait.
2. If the funnel stalls below five, the honest options are to lower the gate to the number actually reached, or to accept a longer calendar. Do not substitute weaker contacts to reach the count. Five wrong-population responses are worse than two right ones, because they look like data.
