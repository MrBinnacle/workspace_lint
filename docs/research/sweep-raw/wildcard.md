**1. The tool's coverage boundary is set by Notion's permission model, not by the tool — and it cannot enumerate what it was never shown.** Notion integrations have access to no pages at first; a user must share specific pages with the integration, and the Search endpoint cannot discover unshared pages. **3. **4. The assurance disciplines solved this by disclaiming completeness, not by certifying it.** ISA 200 defines reasonable assurance as "high, but not absolute," names audit procedures as sample-based and non-exhaustive, and singles out completeness as among the hardest assertions. Copying their artifact structure yields a limitations paragraph, not a differentiator. · SOURCE (ISA 200, Overall Objectives of the Independent Auditor / IAASB / 2013 Handbook / https://www.iaasb.org/_flysystem/azure-private/publications/files/A008%202013%20IAASB%20Handbook%20ISA%20200.pdf) · ATTACKS BELIEF 3 · **CONTEXT-ONLY**

**5. eDiscovery ruled on this exact question and the answer is "reasonableness, not perfection."** Sedona holds an eDiscovery process "need not be perfect, nor even the best available option, and it does not have to identify all discoverable ESI"; FRCP 26(g) requires only reasonable inquiry. A tool selling a certified non-coverage boundary is answering a question the nearest legal-evidentiary discipline explicitly declined to require. · SOURCE (Commentary on Defense of Process / The Sedona Conference / 2016 / https://www.ediscoverycouncil.com/sites/default/files/The%20Sedona%20Conference%20Commentary%20on%20Defense%20of%20Process_Public%20Comment%20Version_Sept%202016.pdf) · ATTACKS BELIEFS 1, 3 · **ADAPT**

**6. Readers demonstrably discount the limitations half of an assurance report.** Audit-report research finds report structure "may lead readers to just retain what is indicated in the opinion paragraph and consider that the paragraph of emphasis merely corresponds to secondary aspects," and that correct decoding requires knowing the technical standard, which is "fairly unlikely." The differentiator is the half readers skip. · SOURCE (audit expectation-gap literature; see also Sirois, Bédard & Bera eye-tracking study / ISAR 2014 / https://www.isarhq.org/2014_downloads/papers/ISAR2014_Sirois_Bedard_Bera.pdf) · ATTACKS BELIEF 1 · **CONTEXT-ONLY**

Its page states no coverage metric. The adjacent market's live product competes on ambient placement, not certified non-coverage. · SOURCE (Stop the Rot: Content Quality for Confluence, on Autopilot / Capable / 2026 / https://www.gocapable.com/blog/stop-the-rot-content-quality-for-confluence-on-autopilot — fetched) · ATTACKS BELIEFS 1, 5 · **ADAPT**

**8. Notion's API rate ceiling is ~3 req/s average regardless of plan, so full-workspace scans are slow by construction.** Per-workspace ceilings scale by plan (reported 10/25/50/100 req/s) but the per-connection 3 req/s average does not. Connectors report 429s merely crawling block children. Full coverage of a large workspace is expensive per run, pushing the product toward sampling — the exact posture that undercuts a completeness claim. · SOURCE (Request limits / Notion Docs / https://developers.notion.com/reference/request-limits — search summary, not fetched) · ATTACKS BELIEFS 1, 5 · **CONTEXT-ONLY**

**The strongest single argument that this project should not be built.**
The differentiator is structurally unreachable on this platform. A Notion integration can see only pages a human explicitly shared with it, and cannot enumerate the rest. The artifact the project sells — a certified boundary of what was not read — therefore collapses to "I read what you gave me," which the customer already knew. Meanwhile the only party who can enumerate the full workspace, the Enterprise owner running Admin content search, already holds a first-party census with CSV export. The tool is weakest exactly where it claims to be strongest, and its claimed buyer already owns the missing half.
